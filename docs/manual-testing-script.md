LoadMate — App Review testing steps

No login required. Test Caravan OR Motorhome. Flow: Settings → Load → Locations → Weight.

CARAVAN
Step 1 — Settings: Select or add a Caravan. Enter kg: MTPLM 1500, MIRO 1350, Weighbridge 1400, Caravan hitch limit 100, Car tow ball limit 100. Tap Save Configuration. Other options (safe zone, weight factors) are optional.

Step 2 — Load: Tap + to add items if needed. Swipe left on a row and tap Load to add; swipe right and tap Unload to remove. Load several items for the trip.

Step 3 — Locations: Drag each loaded item onto a zone on the diagram above, or tap the row to choose a zone. Place items in different zones.

Step 4 — Weight: Review total vs MTPLM, estimated nose/tow ball vs 100 kg limits, and any warnings. Change loads or locations and confirm the summary updates.

MOTORHOME
Step 1 — Settings: Select or add a Motorhome. Enter kg: MAM 3500, MRO 3100. Axle weighbridge: Front 1880, Rear 1980, Max front 1890, Max rear 2000. Tap Save. Optional: rear garage limit; turn on “I have a tow bar” and enter tow bar weight on Load.

Steps 2–4 — Same as Caravan (Load, Locations, Weight). On Weight, check MAM, front/rear axle vs limits, and optional garage/tow bar results.

PASS: App launches without crash. Setup saves. Swipe load/unload works. Locations assign via drag-drop. Weight summary reflects inputs. Data persists after background and relaunch.
