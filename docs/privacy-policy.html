Load Mate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 May 2026

Load Mate (“the App”) is published by Curley Brackets Engineering. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary

All data stays on your device. The App does not require an account, does not use a backend server, and does not upload your caravan, vehicle, loading, or checklist data to us or any third party.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your iPhone or iPad using on-device storage only:

  • Caravan and vehicle setup values (for example MIRO, weighbridge weight, MTPLM, and tow-ball limits)
  • Load items you create (names, weights, and optional in-caravan locations)
  • Items currently loaded for a trip
  • Checklist sections, groups, and activities
  • Whether you have accepted the in-app disclaimer

This information is used only to provide App features such as load calculations and checklists.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The App does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Accounts and sign-in

The App does not require sign-in and does not create user accounts.

Internet access

The App is designed to work offline. It does not need an internet connection for core features and does not send your data over the network.

Children

The App is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it within the App or remove the App from your device. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored App data from that device.

Calculations and disclaimers

Load Mate provides estimated loading and nose-weight calculations for planning purposes only. Always verify actual weights before towing. See the in-app disclaimer for full details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.
