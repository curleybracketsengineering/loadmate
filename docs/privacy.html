Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

LoadMate is provided by Curley Brackets Engineering. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Data collection

LoadMate does not require user accounts and does not collect personal information.

  • No account registration is required.
  • No personal information is sent to Curley Brackets Engineering.
  • No advertising tracking is included.

Syncing between your devices (iCloud)

LoadMate can keep your data in sync across your own Apple devices (for example, iPhone and iPad) when you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID.

Sync uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit. Your app data is stored in your personal iCloud account — not on servers operated by Curley Brackets Engineering. We do not receive, access, or store your synced data.

Data that may be synced includes:

  • Vehicle profiles (caravan or motorhome settings and weight limits)
  • Trips and loaded items
  • Your item library
  • Checklists
  • App preferences stored in the app (such as your active profile)

Sync happens automatically in the background when iCloud is available. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or iCloud is unavailable, LoadMate continues to work using data stored on that device only.

You can control iCloud for LoadMate in your device's Settings → Apple ID → iCloud. Disabling iCloud for LoadMate stops syncing; data already on each device remains on that device.

iCloud sync is subject to Apple's privacy practices. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles iCloud data.

Local storage

Information you enter into LoadMate is stored on your device. When iCloud sync is enabled, a copy of that data is also stored in your personal iCloud account so it can appear on your other devices. Users are responsible for managing their own device backups and data retention.
